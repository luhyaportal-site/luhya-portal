      About Us

      Connecting our past, documenting our present, and empowering our future generations.

      Luhya Portal is a community-powered news and heritage platform for the Mulembe Nation. We report daily from Kakamega, Bungoma, Vihiga, Busia and Trans Nzoia, and we work with elders, historians and families to record the histories of our 18 sub-tribes before they are lost.
